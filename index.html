I am a doctoral researcher at Aalto University, in the Wireless Signal Processing research group at the Department of Information and Communications Engineering, as part of the FOCAL Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network. My research focuses on 6G Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTNs), spanning AI-based resource allocation, network slicing, load balancing via free-space optical inter-satellite links, and interference analysis in multi-layer optical LEO constellations.

Previously, I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the Multimedia Communication and Systems Lab (MCSL), University of Ulsan, South Korea, where I developed metaheuristic optimization frameworks for wireless-powered IoT networks, IRS-assisted systems, and physical layer security.

Research

Multi-layer Non-Terrestrial Networks

AI-based resource allocation, load balancing and interference analysis across LEO, MEO and GEO layers for 6G access.

6G NTNLEO / MEO / GEOResource allocation

Optical Inter-Satellite Links

Free-space optical ISLs that relay traffic between satellites, so load spreads across the constellation instead of queueing at one gateway.

FSOISL routingLoad balancing

Slicing for Time-Sensitive Traffic

Hop-by-hop reservation of time-frequency resources, so delay-critical flows meet their end-to-end delay budget over multi-hop LEO paths.

Network slicingE2E delayMulti-hop LEO

Wireless-Powered IoT and IRS

Energy harvesting, IRS-assisted links and physical layer security, optimized with particle swarm and genetic algorithms.

A. Afridi, A. A. Dowhuszko, R. Wichman, J. Hämäläinen · IEEE GLOBECOM 2026 Workshops, Macau

Time-frequency resource blocks are reserved on every inter-satellite link of a flow's route and aligned hop by hop for pipelined forwarding. At high load and a high reliability target, the scheme needs about 31 ms, 2.5 to 6.8 times less than FIFO, FSF and pure TDMA.
